We calculate the intelligent balance between phases of intensive preparation and recovery as the key to efficiency.

Mission of Xcortex: Creating a fault-tolerant digital ecosystem that ensures a continuous cycle from basic training of young specialists to the deployment of complex automated control systems and physical system modeling.
